# Support FAQ

## Resetting a customer's password
To reset a customer's password, open the Admin Console, search for the customer by email,
and click "Send password reset link." The customer receives an email with a link that
expires after 24 hours. If the customer does not receive the email, check their spam
folder first, then verify the email address on file is correct before resending.

## Cancelling a subscription
Subscriptions can be cancelled from the Billing tab of the Admin Console. Select the
customer's account, click "Cancel Subscription," and choose whether the cancellation
should take effect immediately or at the end of the current billing period. Refunds for
immediate cancellations are processed automatically within 5 business days.

## Exporting customer data
Customer data can be exported as a CSV from the Data tab. Exports include all fields
visible in the customer's profile plus their activity history for the last 90 days. Large
exports (over 10,000 rows) are emailed as a download link instead of generated instantly.

## Escalating a technical issue
If a customer reports a bug that cannot be reproduced or resolved within the support
console, escalate it to the engineering on-call channel with the customer ID, a
description of the issue, and any relevant screenshots or error messages. Engineering
aims to respond to escalations within 4 business hours.
